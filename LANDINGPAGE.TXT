Problem (Why Duevy)
Every semester, the same story.
A rep collects dues in cash or scattered transfers. No receipts. No records. Students ask "where did our money go?" and there's no clean answer. Reps get accused of eating the money. Trust breaks down.
Duevy fixes the record. Every payment is logged, every payout is approved, and every student can see it.

Solution (What Duevy does)
Three short value blocks:
Wallet-based collection — Students top up their Duevy wallet, then pay dues, levies, and fees in seconds. No chasing transfers or counting cash.
Transparent by default — Every student sees what was collected, who has paid, and what the money was spent on. No more "trust me."
Reps stay accountable — Every department gets its own verified payout account, and every withdrawal is logged and traceable back to who requested it.

How it works
One simple flow, from set-up to settled.

Rep Setup their dept.  — Names it (e.g. "200L CSC Department "), sets the team and roles.
Students get the link — Shared on WhatsApp or the class group. No app to install to pay.
Students top up & pay — Fund the Duevy wallet, pay in seconds, get an instant receipt.
Payouts are verified — Funds move only to the department's own verified payout account.
Money moves, cleanly — Funds release to the verified destination. Full trail kept for handover.

FAQ

Do students need to download an app? No but they hace the option to add shortcut on their phone app list. Reps get a full dashboard.
How is my money kept safe? Student funds sit in a separate, partitioned account from platform revenue, on Bachs's secure rails.
Can a rep run away with the money? No. Every department has its own verified payout account, and every withdrawal is logged and traceable back to who requested it.
How do you know a payer is a real student? Every account is verified by matric number through an admin approval queue.
What does it cost? 3% per transaction
Which schools can use it? Duevy is launching at LAUTECH and is built to expand to any Nigerian university.
