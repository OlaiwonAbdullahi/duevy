# Duevy

**Pay your dues. Simply.**

Duevy is a simple way for a class or department to collect money from students online — dues, handouts, levies, manuals — and for students to pay all of it at once, from their phone, in under a minute.

## The problem it solves

Today a course rep drops an account number in a WhatsApp group and then spends weeks collecting screenshots, matching names to transfers, and answering "have you seen my payment?" Students pay four separate times for four separate items. Nobody ends up with a clean record, and when someone asks where the money went, there is no easy answer.

Duevy replaces all of that with one link, one payment, and one list that updates itself.

## How it works

### For the course rep

1. **Sign up.** We check that you really are the rep for your class before approving you.
2. **Verify yourself once with your BVN.** This opens a bank account in your class's name.
3. **Add your dues and share your space code.** Watch payments land, and withdraw to your bank whenever you like.

### For the student

1. **Sign up and enter the space code** your rep gives you.
2. **See everything you owe in one list.** Tick the ones you want to clear.
3. **Make one transfer** to the account number we show you. Your receipt arrives on the spot.

## What classes use it for

Departmental dues · handouts and lecture materials · exam and lab levies · departmental wear · trip and excursion fees · association dues · clearance fees — anything a class currently collects by hand.

No screenshots. No "I've paid o". The rep sees who has paid the moment they pay, and the student never has to prove it twice.

## What it costs

- Students pay a 2% service charge on top of what they owe. On a ₦5,000 due, that is ₦100.
- The class receives the full amount. A ₦5,000 due stays ₦5,000 — the charge is never taken out of the money you collect.
- Reps pay ₦100 to withdraw to their bank account, taken from the amount being sent.
- Nothing else. No signup fee, no monthly fee, and nothing to pay in a month where nobody pays.

## Is the money safe?

- The money is not kept by us. Every class gets its own real bank account, held through a licensed banking partner — the same rails your bank app uses.
- Only the verified rep can take money out, and only into a bank account in their own name. Not a friend's, not a stranger's.
- Every payment leaves a trail. Reference number, timestamp, receipt. Nothing can quietly go missing, and nothing depends on someone remembering.
- Students share nothing sensitive. No card details, no bank password — just a normal transfer, the way they already send money.

## What you actually get

### If you're the rep

- A live list of who has paid and who has not.
- One place your records live, all semester.
- Money in your bank account in minutes, not at the end of term.
- Something to point at when people ask questions.

### If you're the student

- One payment clears everything you owe.
- A receipt the moment your transfer lands.
- Proof you paid, kept for you, forever.
- No group chat begging for confirmation.
